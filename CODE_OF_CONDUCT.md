# Community code of conduct

STEER welcomes contributors across disciplines, organizations, experience levels, and
backgrounds. We expect professional, respectful, evidence-seeking collaboration in every
project space: issues, pull requests, Buzz channels, reviews, events, and private
maintainer communication.

## Expected behavior

- Discuss ideas and evidence without attacking people.
- Make room for questions, dissent, and correction.
- State uncertainty, conflicts of interest, and the limits of agent-generated claims.
- Respect privacy, access boundaries, attribution, and confidential reports.
- Accept human gate authority and do not pressure reviewers to bypass controls.
- Help make the project accessible and safe for new contributors.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized conduct, doxxing, deliberate deception,
credential sharing, retaliation, and sustained disruption are not accepted. Using an AI
agent to produce prohibited conduct does not transfer responsibility away from the human
or organization operating it.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report ordinary conduct concerns privately to the repository owner until a dedicated
community address is published. Report security matters through a private GitHub
Security Advisory. Do not expose personal or exploit details in a public issue.

Maintainers may warn, limit participation, remove content or access, or ban a participant
according to severity and pattern. Reports will be handled as privately and fairly as
practical. A maintainer with a conflict must recuse from the decision.
